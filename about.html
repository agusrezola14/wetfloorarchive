Wet Floor Archive is a visual archive dedicated to the collection and study of “wet floor” signs from around the world. This project stems from my interest in observing the environment and how design influences our daily perception. Through this photographic research, I transform the ordinary into an object of study, exploring the intersection of communication, functionality and aesthetics in public spaces.

More than a simple collection, it is an experiment on graphic identity: each sign, though designed to warn, also reveals cultural traits, graphic patterns and typographic decisions that change with context. In a world saturated with visual stimuli, this project reclaims the everyday as an object of analysis and reflection. Design is not only found in grand graphic pieces or advertising; it also lives in warning signs, caution symbols, and the small visual gestures that guide us without us even noticing.
